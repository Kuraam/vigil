## Canadian Space Agency (CSA)

- **Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health (2026)**
  https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp
  Used in the radiation panel: "Neutrons make up about 30% of total radiation on the ISS. The CSA is developing CANS, an autonomous neutron spectrometer, to monitor this exposure."

## Clinical guidelines and research

| Source | Where VIGIL uses it |
| --- | --- |
| AHA/ACC 2017 blood pressure categories | Blood pressure nominal, watch, action and crisis tiers |
| American Heart Association, low blood pressure | Low blood pressure (watch) |
| American Heart Association: 60-100 bpm resting | Resting heart rate nominal range |
| American Heart Association, how to measure blood pressure | "How to measure" in the blood pressure protocol |
| American Diabetes Association, Standards of Care | Blood glucose tiers |
| American Diabetes Association, 15-15 rule | Low blood sugar protocol |
| CDC fever definition, 100.4 F / 38.0 C | Fever (action) and the fever protocol |
| Clinical definition of hypothermia | Low core temperature (action) |
| WHO Pulse Oximetry Training Manual (2011) | SpO2 tiers and how to measure |
| AASM/SRS 2015 consensus: adults need 7+ hours | Sleep tiers |
| Barger et al., 2014, Lancet Neurology | Sleep context note |
| Stahn et al., 2017, Scientific Reports | Core temperature context note and fever protocol |

Tiers that published figures do not cover are labelled "VIGIL triage rule (team-defined, not clinically validated)".
